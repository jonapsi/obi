- **\# Problem & context**
-
- Clinical psychologists lack dedicated software for their specific workflows. Currently, mental health professionals are forced to adapt generic medical apps designed for physicians. Because of this, they miss out on critical features like functional behavioral analysis tools and scheduling systems built specifically for therapy sessions.
-
- Obi is a purpose-built platform that helps mental health professionals manage their appointments and patients' clinical histories. It is a practical user-friendly tool equipped with exactly what psychologists need to optimize their practice.
-
- **\# Goals**
- **\#\# V1 Success Criteria**
-
- \- **\*\*Platform\*\***: Web application capable of supporting 20+ concurrent users.
- \- **\*\*Access Control\*\***: Role-based access for Administrators, Therapists, Trainee, Supervisor and Receptionists.
- \- **\*\*UX/UI\*\***: An intuitive, practical interface where appointment booking takes ≤ 5 clicks in a calendar-based system for scheduling, rescheduling, and tracking appointments. Includes conflict detection.
- \- **\*\*Clinical Documentation\*\***: Frictionless session note entry featuring modular tools (ABC analysis, mood logs, interventions plans, goal setting/tracking, and free notes).
- \- **\*\*Compliance & Tracking\*\***: In-app alerts for pending session notes and pending supervisor reviews. Automated email and push messages are planned as the first feature after the MVP.
- \- **\*\*Patient Records\*\***: Customizable clinical histories (e.g., learning history, family history, reason for consultation) with file upload capabilities.
- \- **\*\*Security & Regulations Compliance\*\***:
- \- Obi is developed in strict compliance with the Mexican NOM-024 regulation for Electronic Health Records, while actively adhering to the core privacy and security principles of HIPAA as a global best practice.
- 1\. Encryption: TLS protocol for data in transit, encryption at rest for database storage, and field-level encryption for specific fields where the team decides it is needed (for example, MFA secrets). Server-side search runs on metadata such as patient name, date and document type, not on the content of clinical notes.
- 2\. Access Governance: Granular, role-based access control (RBAC) ensuring strict separation of privileges between administrators, therapists, trainees, supervisors and reception staff.
- 3\. Audit Trails: Immutable, timestamped logging of all data access and modification events to ensure clinical traceability.
- 4\. Session Management: Auto-lock and secure timeout mechanisms for inactive client sessions.

- **\# Target Users**
-
- \- **\*\*Primary users\*\***:
- \*\*Clinical psychologists & therapists\*\*
-
-
- Focus: Require specialized tools for functional behavioral analysis, session notes, and treatment planning, rather than generic medical software.
- \- \*\*Clinical Training Roles\*\*: \*Trainees & Supervisors\*
- \- \*Trainees (Students & Junior Therapists)\*
- \- \*\*Focus\*\*: Need full therapeutic capabilities to manage cases and write clinical notes, but within a guided workflow where their documentation is routed to an assigned supervisor for mandatory review and approval. The note's header will automatically display the trainee's name and training level, together with the name and cédula profesional of the overseeing supervisor.
- \- \*Clinical Supervisors\*
- \- \*\*Focus\*\*: Require oversight access to review and approve the clinical notes generated by their assigned trainees. This ensures quality of care, academic feedback, and institutional compliance by permanently linking the supervisor's credentials to the trainee's documentation, without requiring complex electronic signature implementations for the MVP.
- \- \*\*Secondary users\*\*:
- \*Psychiatrists\*
-
- Focus: Many mental health clinics operate with multidisciplinary teams where psychiatrists and psychologists collaborate. They need a unified, compliant platform to share patient histories, align psychiatric and therapeutic treatments, and eliminate the friction of using fragmented or disparate tools.
-
- \- **\*\*Support Staff\*\***: \*\*Receptionists & Clinic Administrators\*\*
-
-
-      Focus: Need a fast, intuitive interface for booking appointments (under 5 clicks) and managing the clinic's schedule without having access to sensitive clinical notes.

- **\# System Requirements**
-
- \- **\*\*Client-side\*\***: Web application compatible with modern browsers (Chrome, Firefox, Safari, Edge).
- \- **\*\*Server-side\*\***: Scalable backend infrastructure capable of handling encrypted data and concurrent user access.
-
- **\# Design Principles**
-
- \* **\*\*Mental Health-Centric:\*\*** Every screen is built around the actual workflow of mental health professionals, stripping away the generic medical clutter found in adapted physician software.
- \* **\*\*Frictionless Action:\*\*** Prioritizing speed and ease of use. Key actions like booking appointments (under 5 clicks) and writing session notes must have zero unnecessary friction.
- \* **\*\*Adaptable Architecture:\*\*** Recognizing that no two clinics are identical, the system provides modular tools (like ABC analysis or custom clinical histories) that users can tailor to their methodologies.
- \* **\*\*Secure by Default:\*\*** Protecting patients ePHI is foundational. All user roles and access controls are strictly bounded to ensure **NOM-024 compliance and HIPAA alignment** without requiring complex setup from the user.
-
- **\# Technical Stack**
- **\- \*\*Frontend\*\*: React paired with TypeScript. This combination guarantees strict type safety and a modular architecture, which is essential for managing complex clinical interfaces.**
- **\- \*\*Backend\*\*: Django 5.2 and Django REST Framework (DRF) 3.16, providing a robust, highly secure, and mature foundation for building scalable RESTful APIs. Authentication and session management are strictly handled via SimpleJWT to ensure stateless, secure access across different clinical roles.**
- **\- \*\*Data Layer (Database)\*\*: PostgreSQL, serving as the primary relational database to securely store patient records and immutable audit logs. Data interactions are managed via the built-in Django ORM, allowing for efficient data handling, advanced querying, and the enforcement of strict database-level constraints.**
